About

From business intelligence to machine learning

I started in Business Intelligence during my bachelor's degree, working with ETL pipelines, data warehouses and dashboards. That foundation shaped how I approach data: it has to be reliable before it can be useful.

My Master's in Decision Support Systems & Data Science took me further into machine learning, natural language processing and generative AI. I enjoy building complete applications around models, so that results are explained and usable, not just accurate.

I'm now looking for a six-month end-of-studies internship in Data Science, Machine Learning or Artificial Intelligence, ideally in a consulting or data-driven environment.
