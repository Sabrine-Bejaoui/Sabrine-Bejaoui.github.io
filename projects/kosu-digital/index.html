KOSU Digital at LEONI

A web application and KPI dashboard that replaced paper-based productivity reporting on LEONI's production lines with real-time monitoring.

Type
End-of-studies internship
Company
LEONI Wiring Systems, Bizerte
Period
Feb – May 2025
Team
Two students

The problem

Productivity on the production lines was measured with KOSU, an indicator comparing the time actually spent with the target time. Data was recorded on paper, which delayed analysis and made it hard to react to waste and performance gaps during a shift.

Objectives

  • Replace paper-based reporting with a digital solution for real-time monitoring of production-line productivity.
  • Centralize production data from several sources in a reliable data warehouse.
  • Let supervisors record output, headcount and incidents hour by hour.
  • Give managers dashboards and alerts to spot waste and performance gaps quickly, in support of Lean manufacturing.

Key features

  • Role-based accessSeparate spaces for administrators, industrial engineering, supervisors and segment managers.
  • Line managementSupervisors enter shift parameters, then adjust headcount and product references hour by hour.
  • Waste (MUDA) trackingSupervisors report a MUDA (breakdown, shortage, staffing, material); a timer measures the downtime until it is resolved.
  • Deviations and actionsDeviations are logged per time slot with the corrective measure taken.
  • Line statusSegment managers see in real time which lines have an open issue and which are resolved.
  • KOSU dashboardFed by the ETL pipeline, with color-coded alerts on the gap between actual and target KOSU, and filters by shift and date.
