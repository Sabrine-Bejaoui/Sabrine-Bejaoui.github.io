IntelliHire

A recruitment web application that extracts candidate profiles from PDF resumes and ranks every candidate against every job offer, with a plain-language explanation of each score.

Type
Academic project
Role
Design and development
Domain
HR and recruitment
Code
Public on GitHub

The problem

Recruiters spend a large part of their time reading resumes and comparing them with job requirements. Keyword filters miss candidates who describe the same skill differently, and scores without explanations are hard to trust.

Objectives

  • Extract each candidate's skills, experience and education automatically from PDF resumes.
  • Score every candidate against every job offer on clear, separate criteria.
  • Explain each score in plain language, with matched and missing skills.
  • Help recruiters compare candidates side by side and track recruitment stages.

Key features

  • Resume parsingText extraction with PyMuPDF, with Tesseract OCR for scanned pages, or manual candidate entry.
  • Profile extractionSkills, experience and education identified with spaCy and a skills dictionary.
  • MatchingLexical (TF-IDF, BM25) and semantic (Sentence Transformers) similarity, with configurable weights and thresholds.
  • Four scoring criteriaSkills match, relevant experience, job requirements match and education relevance.
  • ExplanationsEach score comes with matched, partial and missing skills in plain language.
  • ComparisonSide-by-side comparison of candidates with a recommendation, and stage tracking per job offer.

My contribution

I designed and developed the application: the resume parsing and NLP extraction, the lexical and semantic scoring, the FastAPI REST API with a PostgreSQL schema managed by versioned migrations, automated tests with pytest, and the React and TypeScript interface.
